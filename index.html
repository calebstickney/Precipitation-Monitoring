    Stage IV or MRMS? Which to use
    Stage IV (Daily tab)
    NWS River Forecast Center totals from radar + rain gauges, checked by forecasters. 4 km grid, 24-h days ending 12Z (8 AM EDT / 7 AM EST), 2016 on, includes normals. Best for official daily and monthly totals, long records and % of normal. Limits: coarser grid, fixed 12Z days, newest day posted mid-morning.
    MRMS (Hourly tab, or Daily with midnight days)
    NOAA's automated national radar mosaic, adjusted to rain gauges (multi-sensor). 1 km grid, hourly, Oct 2020 on, any start time including midnight-to-midnight days. Best for storm timing and intensity, small watersheds and the last few hours. Limits: no human review, so radar errors (mountains, far from a radar, snow) can slip through; no normals; continental US only.

    The two can give somewhat different totals for the same place and period.

    Each day is the 24 h ending 12:00 UTC on that date.
